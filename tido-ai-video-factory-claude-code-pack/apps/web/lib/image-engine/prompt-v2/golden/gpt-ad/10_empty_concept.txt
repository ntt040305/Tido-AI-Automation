## S1 — BIG IDEA

Hai Mươi shown plainly and well, with one idea and nothing competing with it

Mood: composed and quiet; addressed to whoever this hero is for, addressed directly and without hype

## S2 — LAYOUT AND ZONES

  - The frame is square.
  - a clear, even margin on every side, wide enough that nothing important reads as touching an edge — nothing that must be read, and no edge of the hero, crosses into it.
  - The subject occupies about half, in the lower third.
  - The words live in the upper third, and nowhere else. No word is placed over the hero.
  - Leave roughly a quarter as quiet, uncluttered ground. Negative space is a decision here, not what is left over.

## S3 — ARRANGEMENT AND DEPTH

  - The hero is a tan leather crossbody bag, and it holds roughly a third. Nothing else competes for that share.
  - three depth layers: something soft in the near foreground, the hero sharp in the middle, and a background that is clearly behind.
  - No overlap is needed: the subject stands clear of everything else.
  - Nothing is deformed, mirrored, stretched or duplicated to fill a gap.

## S4 — CAMERA

  - the lens level with the subject's mid-line, the honest eye-level view
  - the camera level, horizon true, no tilt
  - a long, gently compressing view that flattens the background towards the subject
  - shallow focus: the hero is crisp and the background falls away softly

## S5 — LIGHTING

  - the key light from the front left, high, so shadows fall back and to the right
  - the light is neutral white, neither warm nor cool
  - soft shadows that keep their detail
  - a narrow rim or edge light separates the subject from the background along one side
  - one light direction governs every highlight and every shadow in the frame; each object has a contact shadow where it meets the surface, and no object casts a shadow in a direction the others do not

## S6 — SET AND PROPS

  - Surface: a plain matte surface a shade darker than the background, with no pattern of its own.
  - Background: a plain field of a warm off-white, close to unbleached paper, falling softly out of focus behind the subject.
  - No added props. If the concept names something, that thing and nothing more; otherwise the subject and the surface are the whole set.

## S7 — COLOUR

  - most of the frame is the field colour — a warm off-white, close to unbleached paper — carrying the background and the surface.
  - roughly a third is the secondary, a soft putty grey: the supporting surfaces and the larger shapes.
  - a small part is the accent, a light tan: emphasis only — a single element, a price card, the tightest highlight.
  - The background is never the product's own dominant colour: the product has to separate from what is behind it.

## S8 — TYPOGRAPHY AND THE TEXT MANIFEST

  - One type family throughout: a clean, high-contrast sans-serif. Never more than two families in the frame.
  - the headline dominant, the supporting line a little over a third of it, the smallest line a third
  - small text sits on a flat card, a plain panel or an untextured area of the surface — never over the product, never over texture, never over a gradient that crosses it
  - Every Vietnamese diacritic is rendered exactly as written, in NFC. No string is added, shortened, translated or corrected.

  THE TEXT MANIFEST — every string that may appear, each exactly once:
    [headline] "Bộ sưu tập mới" — at the top of the text zone, first in the reading order, tall enough to read without effort at a glance — never thinner than a hairline against the background, set in a light tan

  Nothing outside this manifest is set. No extra numerals, no invented strap line, no watermark.

## S9 — REALISM AND FINISH

  - every object meets the surface with a contact shadow, darkest where they touch and softening outward, plus a faint reflection where the surface is glossy
  - focus falls away outward from the hero rather than uniformly across the frame
  - micro-imperfections, asymmetric: a droplet or drip that is not mirrored, a crumb out of line, real variation in the material
  - true scale between objects — each the size it would really be beside the others
  - no waxy, plastic or over-smoothed surfaces, and no HDR halo on any edge
  - Finish: a gentle S-curve in the tone, highlights rolling off softly rather than clipping, blacks that keep detail, and no sharpening halo on any edge.

## S10 — CONSTRAINTS

  - no text, numeral, word or character other than the strings listed
  - no logo, wordmark, emblem or icon other than the one the print rule allows
  - no second or duplicate copy of any product
  - no watermark, no signature, no frame or border drawn inside the image
