BLOCK 4 — LIGHT
LIGHT — 1 source at 5300K.
Key: a source 2.3x the product's own width, 34 degrees above the product's centre and 50 degrees camera-left of the lens axis. Key to fill 2:1.
No second source: the fill is bounce off the set itself, keeping the shadow side 2:1 down from the key.
Shadow: soft edge. Contact shadow defined but not deep, holding the product on the surface.
Speculars: at most 1 on the product, each one reading as the same source reflected.
Air: clear. No haze, no atmospheric veiling, no light shafts.

BLOCK 5 — LENS
LENS — 85mm at f/8, macro framing.
Camera: 7 degrees above the product's centre, looking slightly down. No tilt; the vertical edges of the frame stay vertical.
The product fills about 3800% of the frame and sits wholly inside it, never cropped.
Depth of field at f/8 holds the product's full depth sharp; everything behind it falls away without becoming an abstraction.
Movement: no movement, but the scene looks recently touched.

ENVIRONMENT — a single material with a visible but quiet grain.

Depth: 2 planes. Horizon middle.

BLOCK 7 — GRADE & MATERIAL
FINISH — this frame is a photograph on 4x5 colour transparency, not a render.
What that medium does: it holds enormous tonal latitude, shows no structure in the midtones, and compresses highlights for a long way before they give up.
Tonality: the darkest value in the frame sits at 12/255 and nothing reaches pure black; 3 stops of highlight rolloff above the key, so bright surfaces compress instead of clipping to white.
Grain: 0.15% of the frame's width, even across the whole frame and present in the shadows as much as the midtones.
Corner falloff: none. The corners are as bright as the centre.
Colour: held in the product's own hue; the neutrals around it stay neutral.
Optical signature: round and quiet, with no edge outlining; no flare or bloom; a specular highlight stays inside its own shape.

SURFACES — the staging surface is 7% gloss; the rest of the set is matte.
Its own micro-texture reads at about 0.3mm, visible where the light rakes across it and nowhere else.
A specular on the product is no wider than 7% of the product's width, and the product's material keeps its true roughness rather than being polished to read as new.
