BLOCK 7 — GRADE & MATERIAL
FINISH — this frame is a photograph on 4x5 colour transparency, not a render.
What that medium does: it holds enormous tonal latitude, shows no structure in the midtones, and compresses highlights for a long way before they give up.
Tonality: the darkest value in the frame sits at 12/255 and nothing reaches pure black; 3 stops of highlight rolloff above the key, so bright surfaces compress instead of clipping to white.
Grain: 0.15% of the frame's width, even across the whole frame and present in the shadows as much as the midtones.
Corner falloff: 2% darker at the corners than at the centre, gradual, with no visible edge to it.
Colour: held in the product's own hue; the neutrals around it stay neutral.
Optical signature: round and quiet, with no edge outlining; no flare or bloom; a specular highlight stays inside its own shape.

SURFACES — the staging surface is 19% gloss; the rest of the set is matte.
Its own micro-texture reads at about 0.4mm, visible where the light rakes across it and nowhere else.
A specular on the product is no wider than 4% of the product's width, and the product's material keeps its true roughness rather than being polished to read as new.
