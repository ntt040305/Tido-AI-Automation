BLOCK 4 — LIGHT
LIGHT — 2 sources at 5200K.
Key: a source 2x the product's own width, 35 degrees above the product's centre and 48 degrees camera-left of the lens axis. Key to fill 4:1.
1 secondary source(s), each at least 4:1 below the key and none of them casting a second shadow of the product.
Shadow: medium edge. Contact shadow defined but not deep, holding the product on the surface.
Speculars: at most 2 on the product, each one reading as the same source reflected.
Air: haze costing about 7% of the frame's contrast, thickest away from the product.

BLOCK 5 — LENS
LENS — 85mm at f/8, close framing.
Camera: 5 degrees above the product's centre, looking slightly down. No tilt; the vertical edges of the frame stay vertical.
The product fills about 38% of the frame and sits wholly inside it, never cropped.
Depth of field at f/8 holds the product's full depth sharp; everything behind it falls away without becoming an abstraction.
Movement: no movement, but the scene looks recently touched.

BLOCK 6 — ENVIRONMENT
ENVIRONMENT — a single material with a visible but quiet grain.
The staging surface reads at about 45% luminance; the backdrop stays even within 10% across its whole area.
Props: at most 2, each one earning its place and none of them touching the product.
Depth: 2 planes. Horizon middle.

BLOCK 7 — GRADE & MATERIAL
FINISH — this frame is a photograph on 4x5 colour transparency, not a render.
What that medium does: it holds enormous tonal latitude, shows no structure in the midtones, and compresses highlights for a long way before they give up.
Tonality: the darkest value in the frame sits at 12/255 and nothing reaches pure black; 3 stops of highlight rolloff above the key, so bright surfaces compress instead of clipping to white.
Grain: 0.15% of the frame's width, even across the whole frame and present in the shadows as much as the midtones.
Corner falloff: 2% darker at the corners than at the centre, gradual, with no visible edge to it.
Colour: held in the product's own hue; the neutrals around it stay neutral.
Optical signature: round and quiet, with no edge outlining; no flare or bloom; a specular highlight stays inside its own shape.

SURFACES — the staging surface is 23% gloss; the rest of the set is matte.
Its own micro-texture reads at about 0.5mm, visible where the light rakes across it and nowhere else.
A specular on the product is no wider than 8% of the product's width, and the product's material keeps its true roughness rather than being polished to read as new.
