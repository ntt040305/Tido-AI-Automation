## J — ASSET PLAYBOOK

POSTER — the viewer is standing still and gives it three to ten seconds.

What it has to win: one idea a person could describe afterwards, and a hierarchy the eye
follows without being told the order.

Hero: the product holds a little over half the frame — dominant, but not so large that the
frame has no air. One focal point only.
Margins: keep a clear breathing margin on every edge; nothing important touches an edge.
Type: a strict hierarchy. The headline is unmistakably first, the supporting line clearly
second, the call to action smallest and last. Never two elements competing to be read first.
Text sits in the calmest area the composition leaves; it is never placed over the product.
Layout for this canvas: a square frame: the subject centred or just below centre, the words in the calm band above it

## M — GOLD EXAMPLE (illustrative only)

status: seed - unproven

OUTPUT: A vertical poster for the cold brew brand Origin Blend, food and beverage, for a shop window. Photorealistic, finished and ready to publish.

REFERENCE IMAGES: Image 1 is the product, an amber glass bottle with a turned wood cap. Keep its exact shape, proportions, colours, materials and label layout; do not redesign it and do not re-letter it.

SCENE & CONCEPT: The bottle stands alone on a dark slate counter in the first light of morning. A few coffee beans rest beside it. Cool condensation beads on the glass. The idea is the quiet minute before the day starts.

SUBJECT ARRANGEMENT: One bottle, slightly below centre, filling a little over half the height of the frame. Nothing else competes with it.

COMPOSITION & LAYOUT: The bottle occupies the lower middle. The upper third is a calm, softly lit wall left deliberately empty for the words. Generous margins on every edge.

LIGHT / CAMERA / MATERIALS: A single soft light from the left rakes across the slate and catches the condensation. Deep shadow on the right side of the bottle. Shallow depth of field so the wall behind falls gently out of focus. Amber glass, brushed wood, cold wet stone.

COLOR & BRAND STYLE: Warm amber against cold grey-blue, nothing else. Restrained and expensive.

TEXT: In the upper third, centred, a bold modern sans-serif headline in warm white: "Khoi dong ngay moi" rendered as "Khởi động ngày mới". Directly beneath it, smaller and lighter: "Cold brew đậm vị, tươi mỗi sáng". Near the lower edge, small: "Đặt ngay". Render every Vietnamese accent exactly. No other text, numbers, watermarks or extra logos.

CONSTRAINTS: No people, no second bottle, no cup, no visible brand other than the one on the supplied label.
