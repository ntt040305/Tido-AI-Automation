## J — ASSET PLAYBOOK

PRODUCT HERO — examined, often enlarged. Its job is desire for a surface.

What it has to win: surface truth under inspection. The material, the edges and the
markings hold up when someone looks closely.

Hero: the product fills most of the frame, centred or close to it, shot straight and clean.
Light: studio lighting. A soft key that models the form, a rim that separates the product
from the ground, and a contact shadow so it sits on something rather than floating.
Label: legible and undistorted, facing the viewer.
Type: minimal or none. A product hero that needs a headline is a poster.
Props: only what the product cannot be understood without.
Layout for this canvas: a square frame: the subject centred or just below centre, the words in the calm band above it
