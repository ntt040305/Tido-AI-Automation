SOCIAL AD — one to three seconds, on a held phone, often at thumbnail size first.

What it has to win: a frame that survives being small and rewards a second look at full
size. One message, one call to action, high contrast.

Hero: the product or the face is immediately legible at a glance. Nothing subtle.
Type: one message. If a second line exists it is plainly subordinate. Large, high
contrast against whatever sits behind it.
Vertical canvases: keep the top strip and the bottom strip of the frame free of text and
logos — the platform's own interface covers them. Put nothing there you need read.
