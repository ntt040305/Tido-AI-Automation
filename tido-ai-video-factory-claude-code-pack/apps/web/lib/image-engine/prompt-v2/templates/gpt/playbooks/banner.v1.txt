WEB BANNER — under a second, while someone scrolls past, often a few hundred pixels wide.

What it has to win: one statement that is legible when small and in greyscale. Separation
and edge cleanliness matter more than atmosphere.

Hero: the subject sits on one side and holds roughly two fifths of the frame.
Space: the opposite side is clean negative space, and that is where the text goes. Do not
fill it with scenery.
Type: one short statement and, if there is one, a call to action. Nothing else.
Contrast: the subject must separate from the ground without relying on colour alone.
