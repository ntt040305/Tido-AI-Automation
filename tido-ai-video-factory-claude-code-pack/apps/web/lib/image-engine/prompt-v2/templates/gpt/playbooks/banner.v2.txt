BANNER — read in under a second, while scrolling past, often delivered a few hundred
pixels wide.

What it has to win: one statement that survives being small and being greyscale.
Separation and edge cleanliness outrank atmosphere here; a beautiful banner nobody can
read has lost.

The hero scale, the margins, the type sizes and the layout for this canvas are already
decided in the sheet. Do not restate them differently here.

What is still worth saying for this channel: contrast at the edges of the subject, because
that is what holds when the image is downscaled. Nothing subtle in the shadows, nothing
important that depends on fine detail surviving.
