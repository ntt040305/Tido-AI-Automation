SOCIAL — one to three seconds, on a held phone, in a feed of other people's pictures.

What it has to win: a frame that survives a thumbnail and rewards a second look at full
size. It has to stop the scroll on shape and colour alone, before any word is read.

The hero scale, the margins, the type sizes and the layout for this canvas are already
decided in the sheet. Do not restate them differently here.

What is still worth saying for this channel: the silhouette. At thumbnail size the picture
is a shape and two colours, so the subject's outline has to be legible with every detail
removed.
