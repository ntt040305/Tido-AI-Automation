UGC — it has to look like a real person took it, on a phone, without a crew.

What it has to win: the belief that nobody was paid for this. The moment it looks lit, it
has lost the only thing it was for.

The hero scale, the margins, the type sizes and the layout for this canvas are already
decided in the sheet. Do not restate them differently here — but read the camera and
lighting blocks as the LOOK to reach, not as a lighting setup to show: a UGC frame that
describes a key and a rim has described a studio.

What is still worth saying for this channel: one light source that could plausibly be in
the room, a framing that is slightly off-centre the way a real hand holds a phone, and the
ordinary clutter of a real surface left where it is rather than arranged.
