PRODUCT HERO — ten seconds or more, often enlarged, sometimes zoomed into.

What it has to win: surface truth under inspection. The product's material, its edges and
its markings have to hold when someone looks closely, because someone will.

The hero scale, the margins, the type sizes and the layout for this canvas are already
decided in the sheet. Do not restate them differently here.

What is still worth saying for this channel: nothing in the frame may be there for
decoration. A hero shot that has been styled is a hero shot that has been weakened — the
product is the whole subject, and the set exists only to let it separate and to give it a
surface to stand on.
