UGC THUMBNAIL — it has to look like somebody's own photograph, not an advertisement.

What it has to win: the credibility of a real snapshot. If it looks art-directed it has
failed.

Treatment: candid, handheld, available light. Real skin texture with pores and unevenness
intact — no retouching, no smoothing, no beauty lighting.
Hero: an expressive face is the hook, with the product held naturally in frame.
Type: three to five words at most, large, high contrast, the way a person captions their
own photo.
Avoid: studio sweeps, perfect symmetry, glossy highlights, anything that reads as styled.
