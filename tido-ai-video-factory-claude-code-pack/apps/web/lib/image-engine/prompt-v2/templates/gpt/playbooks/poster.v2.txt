POSTER — the viewer is standing still and gives it three to ten seconds.

What it has to win: one idea a person could describe afterwards, and a hierarchy the eye
follows without being told the order.

The hero scale, the margins, the type sizes and the layout for this canvas are already
decided in the sheet. Do not restate them differently here: a playbook that disagrees with
the sheet gives the renderer two layouts to choose between.

What is still worth saying for this channel: the second look has to reward the first. A
poster is approached, so an edge, a material or a small true detail that only reads from a
metre away is worth the frame it costs.
