ASSET: poster at {{ASPECT_RATIO}}.
The viewer gives it three to ten seconds, standing still.

What it has to win: one idea a viewer could still describe three seconds after
looking away, and a hierarchy the eye follows without being told the order.

Subject: the subject fills roughly half the frame and is unmistakably the thing the
picture is about.

Layout for this ratio: {{LAYOUT}}

Copy budget: at most 3 separate lines of text — a headline of at most 8 words, a
supporting line of at most 15 words, and an action line of at most 4 words.

- The words sit inside the scene, in a quiet area the light leaves, never on a panel
  laid over the picture.
- One element dominates; nothing else competes for attention with it.
