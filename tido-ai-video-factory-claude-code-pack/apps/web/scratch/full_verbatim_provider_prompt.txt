- Colour signals: Warm stone tones ΓÇö travertine ivory, sand, pale ochre ΓÇö against the cool blue-green of the Centella leaf. The bottle's own colour anchors the palette. No added green props or foliage beyond the single stem: restraint signals confidence.

THE ROUTE TAKEN: Took the quiet-authority route ΓÇö still life as specimen, not still life as mood ΓÇö over the sensory-ritual route, which the brand cannot own over established spa competitors.
