## REFERENCE SEMANTICS
Reference images are evidence of PRODUCT IDENTITY, not source canvases.

Interpret them as information about a physical product. Do not treat source-image pixels, crop boundaries, background, studio illumination, camera angle, shot distance, perspective, cast shadows, reflections, exposure, white balance, sharpness or depth of field as protected product identity. Do not composite or preserve the source photograph as a visual layer; re-render the product inside the new scene while staying faithful to reference-supported identity.

**Protected (what the product is):** geometry, structural proportions, container form, logo, label text, authentic colours, materials, liquid identity, toppings and garnish.

**Re-synthesized (how it is photographed):** camera angle, viewpoint height, framing, crop, perspective, hero scale, light direction, environmental reflections, contact and cast shadows, ambient colour response, focus.

**Multi-product isolation:** each resolved PRODUCT_xx is an independent identity bound to its own reference evidence. Preserve each one's distinct differences. Do not average identities into a hybrid, do not clone one identity to satisfy another's instance count, and do not transfer features, materials, colours, labels, logos or liquids across identities. Correct quantity with wrong identities is an incorrect output.

**Scene-native integration:** re-synthesize highlights, reflections, refraction, contact shadows, ambient colour, depth, focus and edge sharpness so every element shares one set of photographic conditions. Avoid any pasted cutout, sticker, collage or separately-lit appearance.

**Single reference policy:** with one reference image, unseen surfaces are reconstructed conservatively without inventing unverified logos, text or structural features.
