# CIOS LEVEL 9 --- KNOWLEDGE DOMAIN MAP v3

## Creative Intelligence Operating System

## Purpose

This document defines the complete knowledge territory required for CIOS
Level 9.

The objective is not to build a prompt library.

The objective is to build an expert decision system that allows the LLM
to reason like:

-   Creative Director
-   Art Director
-   Designer
-   Photographer
-   Marketing Strategist
-   Producer

Core transformation:

    Knowledge = Information

into:

    Knowledge = Contextual Expert Decisions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Knowledge Architecture Principle

Every knowledge domain must contain:

-   Facts
-   Decision rules
-   Context conditions
-   Use cases
-   Avoid cases
-   Trade-offs
-   Examples
-   Anti-patterns

A professional does not only know what something is.

A professional knows:

-   When to use it
-   Why to use it
-   When not to use it
-   What alternative exists

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# DOMAIN DEPTH STANDARD (NEW)

Every domain in CIOS Level 9 must not only define what knowledge is

Each domain must contain:

# KNOWLEDGE SCALE REQUIREMENT

Each domain should be expanded based on:

## Level 1 --- Fundamental Knowledge

Basic concepts and terminology.

## Level 2 --- Professional Knowledge

Industry-standard methods.

## Level 3 --- Expert Knowledge

Decision frameworks used by senior creatives.

## Level 4 --- Agency Knowledge

Campaign examples, patterns, failures and strategic applications.

## Level 5 --- Creative Intelligence

Ability to choose, combine and adapt knowledge based on context.
